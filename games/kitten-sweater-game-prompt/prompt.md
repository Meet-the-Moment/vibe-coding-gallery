THE GAME: "Which Sweater Fits Which Kitten?"
The Kitty Crochet Collective is a made-up rescue that knits tiny sweaters for shy kittens. A row of kittens is waiting. A basket of sweaters sits below them. The player puts the right sweater on each kitten.

WHO PLAYS
Families passing one phone around, ages 6 and up. It has to work with a finger on a phone and with a mouse on a laptop.

HOW A ROUND WORKS
- Each kitten shows two clues you can SEE, not read: its body size (small, medium or large) and a thought bubble with the pattern it wants (stripes, dots, hearts, zigzag or plain).
- Each sweater has a size and a pattern you can see at a glance. Draw the patterns big and bold, and give each sweater in a round a different color.
- Tap a sweater to pick it up (it lifts and glows). Then tap a kitten to try it on. Dragging may also work, but tapping must work.
- Right sweater: it slides onto the kitten, the kitten does a happy wiggle, and a few hearts float up.
- Wrong sweater: the kitten shakes its head, and the sweater hops back to the basket. No scolding.
- Round 1 has 3 kittens and 3 sweaters. Round 2 has 4 and 4. Round 3 has 5 kittens and 6 sweaters, so one sweater is a decoy.

THE ONE RULE THAT MATTERS
Every kitten must have exactly one sweater that fits, and the player must be able to tell which one by looking. Build each round by choosing the kittens first, then making each kitten's sweater from its size and pattern. No two kittens in a round may share both size and pattern. The decoy must differ from every kitten in size or pattern. Make the three sizes clearly different on a small screen.

SCORING AND ENDING
- 2 points for a match on the first try, 1 point after a miss.
- Show the score and the round number at the top the whole time.
- After round 3, show the total out of 24, a line of praise from the kittens, and a "Play again" button that deals new kittens.

LOOK AND FEEL
- Draw the kittens and sweaters with simple shapes (SVG or CSS). Give each kitten a different fur color and a name tag: Pepper, Biscuit, Mochi, Clementine, Dot, Waffles.
- Warm and cozy: soft colors, rounded shapes, big tap targets (at least 48 pixels).
- Short, gentle animations. A start screen with the title, one sentence of rules and a "Start" button.
- No sound needed. No sign-in, no names typed in, nothing saved.
